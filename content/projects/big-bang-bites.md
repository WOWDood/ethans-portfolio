## Project

I developed Big Bang Bites for Asas Pembudayaan Keusahawanan (APK), my semester 3 entrepreneurship course. Customers can browse the menu, customise items, and place orders for pickup or delivery. Administrators can manage the menu, orders, and delivery settings.

## My contribution

I built the website in Laravel and used Livewire 4 components for the interactive ordering flow. I designed it for commercial use.
